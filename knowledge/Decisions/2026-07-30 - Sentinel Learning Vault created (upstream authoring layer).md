# 2026-07-30 — Sentinel Learning Vault created (upstream authoring layer)

A dedicated Obsidian vault at `knowledge/sentinel-learning/` was created as the **authoring and research layer** for the Sentinel Brain. It stores trading concepts, strategy analysis, book-derived insights, psychology patterns, and AI-generated summaries that will eventually be ingested into the Brain.

## What was decided

The vault is **upstream** of the Brain, never runtime:

```
Trading Books → Vault (authoring) → knowledge-base/ (ontology) → Brain (runtime)
```

- The Brain (Postgres + pgvector + Knowledge Graph + Concept Learning Engine) remains the sole runtime knowledge source — unchanged
- The vault becomes the source of knowledge that feeds the Brain through an ingestion pipeline (Phase 2, not yet implemented)
- The existing `knowledge-base/` YAML ontology (66 concepts, 15 domains, 13 relations) remains the canonical structured concept store — the vault is upstream of it, not a replacement
- The engineering vault (`knowledge/`) at the parent level remains untouched and separate

## Why this exists

Sentinel's Brain had no authoring surface. The `knowledge-base/` YAML files are the canonical structured store, but they're a destination — there was no research, drafting, review, or book-processing layer feeding them. Trading books existed at `docs/Trading Books/` (14 PDFs) with no pipeline to extract and refine their knowledge into Brain-compatible concepts.

The vault fills this gap as a continuously growing workspace where:
- Books are processed into original summaries and concept drafts
- Trading psychology, institutional concepts, and strategy patterns are documented
- AI-generated summaries are stored for human review
- Concepts graduate through a review process before promotion

## What was explicitly rejected

- **Runtime Obsidian vault** — Sentinel reading markdown files at serve time. Contradicts [[2026-07-17 - Obsidian Knowledge Layer adopted]] which established the Brain as Sentinel's runtime knowledge source. The vault is never wired into the production service.
- **Replacing `knowledge-base/`** — the YAML ontology with its schema validation, directive-language lint, and 13-relation vocabulary remains the structured concept store. The vault is upstream research, not a replacement.
- **Auto-promotion** — concepts generated by AI or extracted from books never automatically become canonical Brain knowledge. Human review is always required.

## Three knowledge systems, three jobs

| System | Location | Audience | Runtime? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Engineering vault | `knowledge/` (parent) | Coding agents | No |
| Sentinel learning vault | `knowledge/sentinel-learning/` | Sentinel knowledge authors | No |
| Concept ontology | `knowledge-base/` | Sentinel Brain (Postgres) | Yes (via `ontology:seed`) |
| Sentinel Brain | `services/sentinel/src/brain/` | Sentinel runtime | Yes |
