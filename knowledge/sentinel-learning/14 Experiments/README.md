# Experiments

Hypotheses, backtesting observations, and empirical notes from testing concepts and strategies against real market data.

Link experiments to the strategy or concept they test. Record both positive and negative results — a strategy that doesn't work under certain conditions is as valuable as one that does.

Sentinel's existing backtesting capability (`npm run backtest:ema` in `services/sentinel`) and Module 12 (Continuous Improvement) can inform notes here.
